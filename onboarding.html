Important to know before you start

Here's how Hocus Focus works for you

Auto-tracks your time Green badge = focus site, Red badge = distraction. Time is tracked automatically in the background.
Nudges you back Every time you open a distraction site, you'll get a gentle reminder to get back on track.
Pin for quick access Click the puzzle icon in Chrome toolbar → find Hocus Focus → click the pin icon. Now it's always visible!
View your dashboard Open the popup and click "View Analysis Dashboard" to see charts, streaks, milestones, and more.
