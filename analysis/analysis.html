    How to Use Hocus Focus

    1

    Add Focus Domains

    Open the extension popup and add websites you use for work or learning — like docs.google.com, khanacademy.org, or github.com. These are tracked as focus time.

    2

    Set Daily Allowances

    Limit distraction sites in the popup. E.g., give yourself 15 min/day on YouTube. You'll get countdown warnings as time runs out.

    3

    Browse Normally

    Hocus Focus tracks time in the background. The badge shows how long you've been on the current site. Green = focus, red = distraction.

    4

    Get Smart Nudges

    When you switch to a distraction site, you'll get a witty nudge. Every 10 min on distractions, messages escalate. Focus milestones get encouragement.

    5

    Check Your Dashboard

    This page shows your focus score, heatmaps, streaks, milestones, and trends. Switch between daily and weekly views to track progress.

    6

    Create Custom Milestones

    Set personal goals like "Focus 3 hours today" or "7-day streak". Track them in the Milestones section with the Add Milestone button.

    7

    Add Your Own Motivation

    Write custom motivational messages at the bottom of this page. They'll show up as nudges when you're on distraction sites.
